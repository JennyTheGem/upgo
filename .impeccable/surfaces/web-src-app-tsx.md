## Direction contract

THESIS: Each project reads like tonight's setlist taped to the stage: the Now set big and numbered under the stage light, the rest of the tour in smaller print, played songs struck out. Refuses the category default of identical KPI cards over a grey list.

OWN-WORLD: Stage black ground, riser and tape greys for panels, one flat magenta stage-light wash (no glow edges), off-white ink. Sections headed by gaffer-tape labels (tilted-free, flat tape strip with the § number). Done is a marker strike, dropped is a VOID-style stamp; nothing disappears. Bars drawn only from real counts, tabular numerals. Dense rows rank by weight and case, not size.

STORY: She opens it and knows at once what is Now everywhere (Tour), drills into a project, sees its counts and progress, filters, reorders, ticks items and copies them to Claude.

Tour view: one setlist block per project with its Now items and counts.
